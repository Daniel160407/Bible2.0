# Made By & Loader

Two tiny shared UI components in `src/components/ui/`.

- **`MadeBy.jsx`** — an author/credit line, shown by [[Action Bar]] and [[Bible Page]].
- **`Loader.jsx`** — a minimal spinner shown while [[Bible Queries (hooks)]]
  requests are in flight (e.g. in [[Search Panel]] / [[Bible Page]]).
